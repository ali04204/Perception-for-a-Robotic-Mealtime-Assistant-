Phase 2 – Dataset target

Number of participants:
- Aim: 10
- Hard minimum: 8

Clips per person:
- Target: 6 clips per person

Total clips:
- Target: ~60 clips total if 10 participants

Minimum transitions per clip:
- At least 1 clear Not Ready → Ready transition in each clip

Clip properties:
- Duration: 8–12 seconds
- Frame rate: ~25–30 fps
- Each clip must contain both not_ready and ready states
